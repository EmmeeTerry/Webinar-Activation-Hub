Quick Start Guide

Choose the right audience

Emails to Customers Emails to Partners

Email templates marked Both appear in both audiences. Sender role does not determine the audience: choose based on who receives the email.

Prepare and send an approved email

  1. Find your email. Select To Customer or To Partner, then search or choose an approved template.
  2. Personalize your message. Update the subject and editable message fields for your contact or account. Use the branded preview to confirm images, links, and layout.
  3. Download formatted Outlook email. This retains the branded HTML layout and embedded images in a downloadable EML draft. Open it in Outlook for Mac or Windows.
  4. Review before sending. Check recipient, greeting, dates, event details, meeting request, links, and image alignment. Send yourself a test first.

If your Outlook version handles EML differently

Some Outlook clients open EML files in read-only mode or handle images differently. Use Copy formatted email to paste into a new Outlook message when necessary, then check every image and link in a test email. Plain-text alternatives are available under More options.

The Hub does not send messages for you. No recipients are added automatically.
