Sales & PAM Email Template Hub

Approved emails to customers and partners | Quick Start Guide

Who is receiving your email?

Sales and PAM teams can use approved email templates to communicate with customers and partners. Choose by recipient, not your own job title.

To Customer To Partner

Templates marked Both appear under To Customer, To Partner, and Both. Sales and PAM team members can use the appropriate approved template regardless of their own role.

Prepare and send an approved email

  1. Choose the recipient. Filter by To Customer, To Partner, or Both, then select an approved email template.
  2. Personalize your email. Edit directly, or select Personalize from contact list. Upload a CSV with First Name and Email columns, choose one contact, then click Prepare Outlook email for this contact. The download includes that contact's email address in the To field and the formatted invitation. Open it in Outlook, review, and click Send. The Hub does not bulk-send messages.
  3. Download formatted Outlook email. This retains the branded HTML layout and embedded images in a downloadable EML draft. Open it in Outlook for Mac or Windows.
  4. Review before sending. Check recipient, greeting, dates, event details, meeting request, links, and image alignment. Send yourself a test first.

Translate while preserving the design

Open Translate above the subject. Try Translate in browser. For an outside translation, expand Use a translator or translation tool instead. Download the CSV translation worksheet, have the Translation column completed using an approved service, then upload the worksheet. The Hub applies the translated wording to the existing email layout, leaving its images and links in place.

Do not upload confidential customer information to an unapproved external translation service. Always review the translated draft before sending.

Import an existing email

Have an approved email already designed? Open Import an email (.eml) in the Hub, choose your file, and select To Customer, To Partner, or Both.

Best format: .eml, with embedded images included. Some .emltpl files may also work if they contain standard email content. .oft, .msg, Word, PDF, and standalone HTML are not supported. If your email app cannot save an .eml file, ask the email owner for one.

After importing, preview and test your formatted email in Outlook. Importing prepares a local draft for this session; it does not publish a template for everyone.

If your Outlook version handles EML differently

Option A, best for branded designs: Click Download formatted Outlook email, then open the downloaded .EML in Outlook. If Outlook opens it read-only, use Option B.

Option B, copy and paste: Click Copy email body for Outlook, then Open Outlook (web) or Open desktop email app. Click in the new message above your signature, paste with Ctrl+V (Windows) or Command+V (Mac), and review the layout before sending.

If SharePoint blocks copying, the Hub opens a separate browser tab with the formatted email and another Open Outlook link. If the tab is blocked too, manually copy the selected email or use the .EML download.

For a selected CSV contact, the downloaded Outlook draft includes their email address in the To field. You review and send it from Outlook. For the original template, no recipient is prefilled.
